Titiloye Daniel Oluwadarasimi — most people just call me DOT.

I'm a brand strategist, social media strategist, content strategist and content writer. For more than four years, I've worked directly with brands, creatives, law firms and businesses across very different industries, from music and tech to construction, legal and education.

I started as an intern at Mind to Media, where I was recognised as a top-performing intern. I was named Best Graduating Student in Digital Strategy & Social Media at Sogilace Tech Brand. Since then, the work has grown from running pages to shaping strategy, leading teams and building things of my own.

Here's what I believe: social media isn't about posting. It's about knowing who you're talking to, what you want them to do, and whether it's working. So I build clear content systems, keep brand communication sharp, and run campaigns that are meant to move something real.

I don't just do the work. I teach it. I've trained more than 200 Social Media Managers, and I'm building The SMer space so more people can learn the craft properly and more brands can stop guessing when they hire.

Social Media Strategy & Management

Content Strategy & Writing

Campaign Planning & Execution

What I care about

The ideas behind the work.

01

Strategy over activity.

A busy page isn't the same as a growing brand. Every post should connect to something the business actually needs.

02

Structure over noise.

Good brands aren't built on random bursts of content. They're built on clear systems that keep the message consistent.

03

Hiring right, not guessing.

Brands deserve to know who's actually qualified. That's a big reason I care about proper training and certification.

04

Giving talent a real path.

Many people learn this work informally and get stuck. I care about turning skill into a career, with a portfolio and credibility behind it.

05

Useful over loud.

I'd rather teach something practical than say something motivational. Clear thinking beats hype.

06

Africa-first.

I'm building from Lagos for the talent and brands around me first, with a vision that goes beyond Nigeria.
